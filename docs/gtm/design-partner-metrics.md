Track **10–20** pilot trips (friends, one wedding, one corporate offsite). Users stay free; goal is proof for India GTM.

## Aggregate (after pilots)

- Median spends per trip
- Claim rate = claimed seats / roster size
- % trips with ≥3 spends
- NPS or one quote per segment
