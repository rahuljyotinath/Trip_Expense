# Money model

## Question

Concrete Y1–Y3 targets we will **actually chase** (not analyst TAM):

- B2B price bands from one-pager
- Optional consumer Plus price vs Splitwise (~₹999/yr India cite)
- ARR milestones: Y1 / Y2 / Y3

Align with who-pays and beat-definition.

## Answer

**Pricing (unchanged bands from one-pager):**

- Operator / trek: **₹2,000–15,000 / month** by active trips or seats
- Corporate offsite: **₹5,000–25,000 / event** or annual pack
- Wedding planner seat: **₹1,000–5,000 / month**
- College: custom / sponsored

**Tripzy Plus (Y2 launch):** **₹499 / year** intro → **₹799 / year** list (undercut Splitwise Pro ~₹999–1,200 India). Free tier remains full split/settle.

**ARR targets (operator-weighted, conservative):**

| Year | Paying operators | Blended ARPU/mo | Plus subs (Y2+) | **ARR chase** |
|------|------------------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Y1 | 8 | ₹8,000 | — | **~₹8L** |
| Y2 | 35 | ₹10,000 | 500 | **~₹45L** |
| Y3 | 80 | ₹12,000 | 3,000 | **~₹1.4Cr** |

Y1 assumes mostly annual-ish contracts and 2–3 corporate events; not TAM slides. Revisit after 10 design-partner trips close ([`design-partner-metrics.md`](../../../docs/gtm/design-partner-metrics.md)).

**Consumer revenue** is upside in Y2+; Y1 quota is **paid operator logos + trip proof**, not Plus MRR.
