## Answer

**Phased product + full-replacement positioning** (not literal feature parity on day one).

- **Public job:** “The Splitwise alternative built for how Indians actually split — trips, dinners, roommates, IOUs — without daily limits or ad clutter.”
- **Y1 product truth:** Trip container covers **most social splits** (Goa, wedding side, office lunch run, flat weekend expenses) via roster + join + settle; one primary currency per trip; equal + percent splits ship today.
- **Explicit Y1 non-goals:** recurring rent utilities, global FX trip, receipt OCR — call these **roadmap**, not launch promises.
- **Y2+ parity path:** standing **groups** (non-trip), multi-currency, recurring bills — same account, migrate a “trip” to permanent group when dates end.

GTM must not claim roommate OS until standing groups ship; **can** claim replacement for **any ad-hoc group expense** because trip UX generalizes to short-lived and long-lived groups.
