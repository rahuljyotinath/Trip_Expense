# Beat definition

## Question

What does **beat Splitwise** mean for Tripzy?

- **Displace** — take their India MAU / App Store rank.
- **Own niche** — default app for India group trips; happy coexisting for flatmates.
- **Monetize wedge** — capture users who hit 4/day limit or ads; convert to Tripzy + optional Plus.
- **B2B bypass** — operators standardize on Tripzy; travelers never choose Splitwise on org trips.

Pick primary success metric for Y1–Y2.

## Answer

**Primary: monetize wedge + B2B bypass.** Secondary: own India **social split** niche (trips + dinners), not global displacements.

**Y1 success metrics (measurable):**

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Paid operator accounts | 5–10 (design partners → first invoices) |
| Consumer MAU (India) | 3,000 (organic + operator-led) |
| Trips with ≥3 group spends | 60% of active trips |
| Splitwise switch stories | 20 documented (support / NPS) |

**Y2 stretch:** top-10 India finance “expense sharing” store keyword; 25k MAU; ₹15L+ ARR operator-heavy.

We **do not** target US/EU displacements or App Store #1 vs Splitwise globally in Y1–Y2.
